# Joy Safari Jaipur — Official Resort & Adventure Website

A multi-page website built for **Joy Safari Jaipur (Venture of Joy, Adventure & Resort)**, Jaipur's first-of-its-kind family & couples adventure resort.

## 🎨 Design System
- **Theme:** Royal Rajasthani Festive Luxury with Dark Night Lake Glow
- **Primary Maroon:** `#7A0F1B`, `#5C0A14`
- **Royal Gold:** `#D4AF37`, `#F5D26B`, gold emboss gradients
- **Dark Night BG:** `#0F0705`, `#1A0A0C`
- **Cream:** `#FFF3D6`
- **Forest Green CTA:** `#0F3D2E`
- **Typography:** Cinzel & Playfair Display (Headings), Poppins (Body)

## 🗺️ Routes & Pages
- `/` — **Home:** Hero matching reference poster, 12 Activities Grid, ₹1999 Package Highlight, Room Stays (₹6999 & ₹7999), Suggested Safari Timeline, Pickup Points, Reviews, FAQs, Contact Strip.
- `/activities` — **Activities:** Filterable (All, Adventure, Water, Culture, Fun, Food) with 12 activity cards, safety ratings, and duration.
- `/packages` — **Packages:** Day Safari Pass (₹1999 + GST), Garden Facing Room (₹6999/-), Lake View Suite (₹7999/-), and comparison.
- `/rooms` — **Rooms:** Detailed luxury room showcase with amenities, views, and breakfast inclusions.
- `/gallery` — **Gallery:** Photo gallery with interactive lightbox modal.
- `/about` — **About Us:** Brand story, "1st Time in Jaipur" concept, Safety standards, and Family dignity promise.
- `/contact` — **Contact:** Booking inquiry form connected to WhatsApp, Direct Call link, official website, and interactive Jaipur map.
